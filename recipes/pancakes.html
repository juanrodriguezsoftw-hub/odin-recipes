Pancakes recipe

Description

An easy recipe that will help you cook the best pancakes for breakfast. Fortunately, you won't need much time to make them.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour.
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar.
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder.
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt.
  • 1 cup milk.
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil.
  • 1 large egg, beaten.

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl; make a well in the center. Pour in milk, oil, and egg; mix until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat. Pour or scoop about 1/2 cup batter per pancake onto the griddle; cook until bubbles form and the edges are dry, 1 to 2 minutes. Flip; cook until browned on the other side. Repeat with remaining batter.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy!
